DataGrail's research reveals that over 63% of websites fail to comply with universal opt-out mechanisms like GPC signals, yet this noncompliance affects less than 2% of visitors. The analysis shows that while regulatory enforcement is intensifying, consumer opt-out behavior remains relatively low, with most users either accepting all tracking or dismissing consent banners. Organizations need continuous monitoring of their consent management configurations to prevent compliance gaps, especially regarding commonly-used tools like Google Analytics and Meta trackers.
