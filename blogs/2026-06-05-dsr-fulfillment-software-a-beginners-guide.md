This guide explains DSR (Data Subject Request) fulfillment software, which automates the operational steps of managing privacy requests from intake through audit logging. The article covers what triggers DSR requirements across different regulations, common automation patterns like zero-click workflows and human-in-the-loop processes, and key metrics for measuring automation success.
