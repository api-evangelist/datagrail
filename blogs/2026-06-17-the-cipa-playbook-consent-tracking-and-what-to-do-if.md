California's 1967 Invasion of Privacy Act (CIPA) wasn't designed for the internet, but over the past three years courts have extended this wiretapping law to cover session replay tools, chat widgets, tracking pixels, and fingerprinting scripts. Plaintiffs' firms have filed thousands of claims as a result.
