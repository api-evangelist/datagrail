A DataGrail webinar gathered privacy leaders to discuss findings from the 2026 Privacy & AI Trends Report. California now has over 100 active privacy investigations and 10,000+ consumer complaints, 63.6% of vendors don't disclose third-party AI subprocessors, and data subject requests have risen 567% since 2021 (Dexcom saw a 20X increase over six years).
