This monthly privacy dispatch highlights three major trends: California courts expanding CCPA's civil right of action to cover tracking pixels, AI deployments increasing privacy risks while outpacing regulation, and data brokers facing mounting pressure through expanded state definitions and enforcement actions like the FTC's settlement with Cox Media Group.
